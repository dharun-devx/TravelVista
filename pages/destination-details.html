GET TO KNOW THE CITY

More than a postcard

Paris rewards the curious. Wander beyond the grand boulevards and you’ll find a city made for lingering: a morning at the market, a bookshop tucked around a corner, a picnic that lasts until sunset.

Give yourself room to explore without a plan. Cross the river, choose a street that looks interesting, and let the day unfold. The best Paris moments are often the ones you didn’t schedule.

Make it your own

Start early for the quietest views, take breaks at neighborhood cafés and leave time to walk. There’s no single right way to see Paris—just the one that feels like yours.
